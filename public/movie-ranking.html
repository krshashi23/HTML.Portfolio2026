The Best Movies According to Shashi

My top 3 movies of all-time.

3-IDIOTS

3-Idiots is a popular Indian film that highlights the pressures of the education system and the importance of learning with passion rather than fear. The story follows three engineering students whose friendship helps them navigate academic stress, personal challenges, and societal expectations. Through humor and emotional moments, the movie delivers a strong message about following one’s dreams, thinking creatively, and defining success on one’s own terms.

Ae Dil hai Mushkil

Ae Dil Hai Mushkil is a romantic drama that explores unrequited love, friendship, and emotional vulnerability. The film follows the complex relationship between Alizeh and Ayan, showing how love is not always mutual or fulfilling. With soulful music and intense performances, the movie highlights the pain of one-sided love and the journey of self-growth that comes from heartbreak.

PK

PK is a thought-provoking Bollywood film that uses humor and satire to question blind faith, religious hypocrisy, and social beliefs. The story revolves around an alien who comes to Earth and innocently challenges human customs and rituals, forcing people to reflect on their actions. Through its simple yet powerful narrative, the movie delivers a strong message about humanity, love, and understanding beyond religious differences.
